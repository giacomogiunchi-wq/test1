# ADR 0011: GPL-3.0 project and adapter boundaries

* Status: Accepted
* Date: 2026-09-19

## Context

The platform needs a durable decision for this concern before feature expansion.

## Decision

Duomec remains GPL-3.0. Preserve dependency notices, prefer compliant dynamic/process integration, and review every binary distribution; boundaries are architectural, not a claim to remove license obligations.

## Consequences

Copyleft obligations are visible and auditable. LGPL and per-file PlaneGCS terms still require packaging verification.
